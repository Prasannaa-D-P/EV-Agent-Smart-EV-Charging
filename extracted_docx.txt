
CHENNAI EV CHARGING STATION DATA
EV-Agent – Agentic AI Powered Smart EV Charging Platform
Purpose: Reference dataset for the EV-Agent prototype, covering EV charging-station locations in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Coordinate-level records are intended as seed data; live charger availability must be verified through current operator/TNEV sources before being represented as real-time.
1. Data Source and Usage Notes
Primary coordinate reference: Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) public EV charging-station dataset.
Current-status reference: Tamil Nadu TNEV / TNGECL charger-finder platform and individual charging-network operator listings.
This document separates location information from live availability. A station being listed at a location does not mean a charger is available at this moment.
Approximate coordinates from secondary listings should not be treated as production-grade geospatial coordinates until independently verified.
For the EV-Agent prototype, store data_source, last_verified_at, and is_demo fields for every station.

4. Data Quality Classification
Use the following classification in the EV-Agent database:
VERIFIED_COORDINATE: Exact latitude/longitude obtained from a coordinate-bearing source.
OPERATOR_LISTING: Station reported by an operator or current directory, requiring coordinate verification if exact coordinates are not supplied.
LIVE_STATUS: Current availability/status obtained from a live or current charging-network source.
ESTIMATED: Value calculated by EV-Agent, such as estimated travel time or charging cost.
DEMO: Simulated data used for development/testing and not presented as live infrastructure.
5. Important Implementation Rule
Do not use approximate coordinates as final production coordinates. Before inserting a station into the production database, verify the exact location and current operational status through TNEV/TNGECL, the relevant charging operator, or another authoritative current source. Store the verification timestamp and source.
