Objectives
• Optimize EV Charging – Determine the best time and method to charge an 
electric vehicle efficiently. 
• Find Available Charging Stations – Recommend suitable nearby charging 
stations based on availability and user requirements. 
• Reduce Charging Cost – Suggest cost-effective charging schedules by 
considering electricity prices and demand. 
• Enable Autonomous AI Decisions – Use Agentic AI to intelligently plan and 
manage charging with minimal user intervention. 
• Improve User Convenience – Provide personalized charging recommendations 
based on battery level, travel needs, and user preferences.

=== PAGE 6 ===
Existing System & Problems
Existing System
• Manual Charging : Users manually select when and where to charge. 
• Basic Charging Apps : Show charging stations and their availability. 
• Fixed Scheduling : Charging is scheduled using predefined time slots. 
• Limited AI : Existing systems provide basic recommendations. 
• Separate Services : Cost, station selection, and charging are handled 
separately .
Problems
• High charging cost during peak hours. 
• Charging stations may be unavailable when users arrive. 
• No personalized charging plans. 
• Limited real-time decision-making. 
• Requires frequent user intervention.
